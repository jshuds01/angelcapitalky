05 · FemTech / BioTech

Simbryo Technologies

📍 OneNKY Center, Covington, NKY  ·  Founded 2023  ·  Commercial Launch Oct 2025

IVF · Endometrial Organoids · FemTech 🟢 Actively Seeking Seed Investment

Simbryo Technologies has launched Simbryo FX — the world's first personalized functional fertility assay that uses a patient's own biopsy tissue to create 3D endometrial organoids, then implants blastoids (stem cell-based embryo models) to quantify endometrial fertility before IVF transfer. Unlike existing tests that check timing or infection, Simbryo FX measures whether the uterus is functionally ready to receive an embryo — addressing the most common cause of costly failed IVF transfers. Commercially launched October 2025, the company is operating from LifeSciKY's wet lab in Covington and was named a 2026 KY Inno Startup to Watch and a 2026 Cincy Inno Fire Awards finalist.
