CleanTech · Advanced Materials
Sofab Inks
📍 Louisville, KY · UofL Conn Center spinout
⚡ New This Month Seed Closed DoE-Supported 🌿 Thesis: Clean Energy

The headline Kentuckiana raise of the summer. Sofab Inks closed a $6M seed round on July 21, 2026, led by strategic investor Cloudberry Ventures. The company makes “Tinfab” metal-oxide nanoparticle inks used as the electron transport layer (ETL) in perovskite solar cells — the layer that has historically caused perovskite modules to degrade. Sofab’s material bonds 6–10× more strongly than the fullerene layer it replaces and has demonstrated 22.3% efficiency on 30‑cm single-junction modules, directly attacking perovskite’s reliability barrier to mass-market adoption.

Kentuckiana Capital Ecosystem

Where the money actually comes from in the Louisville / NKY / Southern Indiana corridor — and what changed this summer.

Keyhorse Capital

Seed-stage fund and initiative of KSTC in partnership with the KY Cabinet for Economic Development. Has financed 336 Kentucky companies (212 active) via the Kentucky Enterprise Fund and KSBCI 2.0, leveraging $112M+ since 2002. Deployed $6.5M across 22 companies in its Q2 2026 cycle. The Q3 2026 cycle opened August 10 and closes September 30 — the single most actionable deadline in this report.

Seed · Quarterly Cycles
Bluegrass Angels

One of Kentucky’s longest-running angel networks, syndicating seed checks into early-stage Kentucky companies. Frequent co-investor alongside Keyhorse and Commonwealth Seed Capital, and a primary route for individual investors to access qualified deals eligible for the state angel tax credit.

Angel Network
Kentucky Angel Collective

Statewide angel investor network launched in June 2025 to provide early funding and mentorship to Kentucky entrepreneurs. Now in its second year and increasingly the connective tissue between Louisville, Lexington and NKY angel pools.

Angel Network
Connetic Ventures

Covington-based firm operating as VCAFX — the first SEC-registered venture mutual fund in the country, with roughly $30M under management across 45 states. A structurally unusual vehicle that gives non-accredited investors exposure to early-stage venture.

NKY · Pre-Seed / Seed
Fireroad Ventures

Northern Kentucky pre-seed fund backing applied AI and automation startups with a hands-on, founder-first model. Often the first institutional money into NKY software companies.

NKY · Pre-Seed / AI
Commonwealth Seed Capital

State-affiliated seed fund making debt and equity investments in early-stage Kentucky companies with high growth potential. Regularly co-invests behind angel syndicates and pairs well with the 40% angel tax credit for individual investors in the same round.

State Seed Fund
eGateway Capital

Louisville-based fund investing in underrepresented and underestimated founders across the Ohio Valley. One of the few regional vehicles with an explicit equity mandate, making it a natural co-investment partner for Angel Capital’s workforce-equity and financial-inclusion positions.

Louisville · Equity Mandate
Render Capital

Louisville’s most prominent early-stage venture fund, writing $750K–$1.25M pre-seed and seed checks across Kentucky, Southern Indiana and the broader heartland — often the first institutional capital a company takes on. Sectors include healthcare, health tech, supply chain/logistics, advanced manufacturing, CPG and B2B SaaS.

Pre-Seed / Seed · $750K–$1.25M
KY Angel Investment Tax Credit

Up to 40% credit on qualified investments in enhanced (high-unemployment) counties, 25% elsewhere. Annual caps: $3M program-wide, $200K per investor, $1M per qualified small business. Both the investor and the business must file a new 2026 application — prior-year certification does not carry over. Contact: ced.angel@ky.gov.

40% Credit · 2026 Applications Open
Vogt Awards

Run by the Community Foundation of Louisville since 2000 and now past its 25th anniversary Demo Day. Provides a $25,000 non-dilutive grant plus a 10-week accelerator — the standard first non-dilutive check for Louisville founders. Due Gooder, Essential Innovations and The Nori Project in this report are all Vogt-backed.

Non-Dilutive · $25K + Accelerator
Blue North / SparkHaus & BRINK

Northern Kentucky’s innovation hub. SparkHaus is a $16M, 51,000 sq ft Covington hub now drawing relocations from California, Texas and North Carolina. BRINK was named a finalist for the 2026 Cincinnati Business Courier Fire Awards, having deployed $430K+ into 15 companies and unlocked $2M+ in follow-on. Shinkle Fellows pays founders $10K plus housing for a 12-week Covington residency.

NKY Hub · Pre-Seed Programs
LifeSciKY / OneNKY Center

Life-sciences convening infrastructure for the commonwealth and the Northern Kentucky corridor. Relevant this month as NKY consolidates a genuine health-tech cluster — Simbryo, Cortechs.ai, Definity and FwdSlash all now operate within a few blocks of each other in Covington.

Life Sciences · NKY
SBIR / STTR + Kentucky Matching Funds

Both federal programs were reauthorized in March 2026 and now run through September 30, 2031, ending the lapse that began September 2025. KY Innovation matches all or part of federal awards to Kentucky-based businesses; eight Kentucky companies shared $862,479 in state matching grants earlier this year. KSEF micro-awards provide up to $5,000 to strengthen an SBIR proposal.

Non-Dilutive · Reauthorized to 2031
Amplify Louisville · Leadership Transition

The Louisville-region innovation hub completed an executive director transition this quarter, marked by a “Passing the Torch” event where the Louisville mayor proclaimed June 30 “Amplify Day.” Worth monitoring: hub leadership changes typically shift which founders get routed to which capital sources over the following two quarters.

Notes, Methodology & Disclaimer

How this issue was assembled and what it is (and is not).

Disclaimer — August 14, 2026

This report was compiled by Angel Capital on August 14, 2026. It is a catch-up edition: the regularly scheduled first-Monday run for August was missed, so this issue deliberately folds in notable activity from both July and early August 2026.

Nothing here is an offer to sell or a solicitation to buy any security, nor is it investment, legal, accounting or tax advice. Figures — including round sizes, valuations, traction metrics, raise status and total capital raised — are drawn from public sources, press releases, ecosystem reports and company-published material, and may be incomplete, delayed or superseded. Several companies profiled are private and disclose selectively; treat all numbers as directional. Early-stage investing carries a substantial risk of total loss of capital. Conduct your own independent diligence and consult qualified professional advisors before investing.

Thesis tagging. The 🌿 badge reflects Angel Capital’s internal common-good screen (clean energy and environmental sustainability; healthcare and patient outcomes; women’s and maternal health; education and learning access; workforce equity; financial inclusion; elder care; food security; social determinants of health and community wellness). It is an editorial judgment by Angel Capital, not a certification, rating or third-party ESG score, and companies do not pay for or approve inclusion.

Tax credit note. The Kentucky Angel Investment Act credit is subject to annual program caps, per-investor and per-business limits, and county-level rate variation (40% in enhanced counties, 25% elsewhere). Both investor and business must file new 2026 applications. Credit availability is never guaranteed — verify current status with the Kentucky Cabinet for Economic Development before relying on it in an investment decision. Indiana-domiciled companies (e.g. Proper Pilots) are not eligible for the Kentucky credit.
